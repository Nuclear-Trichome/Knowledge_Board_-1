Voozr P2 A Pen - Temperature Ladder (Lowest -> Highest)
=======================================================

NOTE ON BOOST:
The P2 A Pen allows one boost per heat cycle.
Estimated boost increase: ~+22°F, slightly above the Pivot's ~+18°F boost.
Boosting is also expected to extend the session slightly; exact added time is not confirmed here.


=======================================================
1. LOW (Blue) [Flavor]
   Optimal flavor, least vapor.

Base ............. 500°F
Boost 1x ......... ~522°F


=======================================================
2. MEDIUM (Green) [Balanced]
   Balanced flavor and vapor.

Base ............. 520°F
Boost 1x ......... ~542°F


=======================================================
3. HIGH (Red) [Strong]
   Higher vapor, maintains flavor.

Base ............. 540°F
Boost 1x ......... ~562°F


=======================================================
4. LIMIT (White) [Power]
   Most vapor, less flavor.

Base ............. 585°F
Boost 1x ......... ~607°F


=======================================================
Relative Order (Low -> High)
1. Blue - Low / Flavor
2. Green - Medium / Balanced
3. Red - High / Strong
4. White - Limit / Power
=======================================================
